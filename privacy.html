Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StreamClipper handles information when the creator uses the app to review livestream recordings, identify highlight moments, and send drafts to their own TikTok account and optionally to YouTube.

1. Overview

StreamClipper is a personal productivity tool for one creator. The app is designed to process recordings and clips from the creator’s own livestreams and to prepare draft posts for the creator’s own accounts.

We do not sell personal data, do not use your information for advertising, and do not share it with ad networks. We only process data needed to make the service function for the creator.

2. Data Accessed from TikTok and from the User

The types of information StreamClipper may access depend on what the creator chooses to connect and use. This may include:

  • basic TikTok account information obtained through TikTok Login Kit, such as user.info.basic;
  • account identifiers needed to connect to the creator’s own TikTok account;
  • video upload or content posting data needed to send a draft to TikTok;
  • recording files, clips, transcripts, captions, and metadata stored locally on the creator’s device or local systems;
  • AI input and output data, such as transcript text or highlight suggestions, when the creator chooses to send data to an AI provider like Anthropic; and
  • optional YouTube account connection details and post metadata if the creator chooses to share content there.

StreamClipper may also process information the creator provides manually, such as notes, tags, prompt text, clip selection choices, and account connection settings.

3. How Data Is Used

We use the information to provide the core service, including:

  • connecting the creator to their own TikTok account and optional YouTube account;
  • reviewing recordings and transcripts to identify highlights;
  • preparing drafts of clips and related metadata;
  • sending clips or draft content to the creator’s chosen account or platform;
  • supporting app operations, troubleshooting, and account management; and
  • handling deletion or access revocation requests.

We do not use creator data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties. The creator’s own decisions about what to upload, post, or delete remain under their control.

If the creator chooses to send a transcript to an AI provider (such as Anthropic) to help identify highlight moments, that transcript is used only to provide that feature and is not used for unrelated analytics or ad targeting by us.

4. Third-Party Processing

Some processing is handled by third-party services, including:

  • TikTok, for login and content posting;
  • YouTube, if the creator opts to use it;
  • AI providers, if the creator chooses to use them to analyze transcript text or select highlights; and
  • hosting, storage, or infrastructure services that the creator runs for the app.

These services are governed by their own privacy terms and security practices. We do not sell or rent the creator’s personal data to third parties for advertising. We also do not use the creator’s data in any promotional or ad targeting system.

5. Storage and Retention

StreamClipper is designed to keep recordings, tokens, clips, and related data only where the creator runs the app. We do not assume any cloud storage or third-party data warehouse is required to operate the service.

The creator controls the local storage location, retention period, and deletion practices for content they store on their own systems. We recommend keeping only the files needed for active work and deleting older recordings and clip drafts when they are no longer needed.

We retain personal data only as long as needed to operate the service, fulfill the creator’s requests, maintain account access, or comply with legal obligations. If the creator revokes access or asks for deletion, we will honor that request as described below.

6. User Choices and Deletion

The creator can choose whether to connect TikTok, YouTube, or AI services, and can disconnect them at any time. For TikTok, access can be revoked in Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services.

If the creator wants their personal data removed from our records or from the associated app process, they can request deletion by email at argintmax12389@gmail.com.

Deletion requests will be handled as quickly as reasonably possible. The creator may also remove files and local data directly from the systems where the app is running, since the service is meant to operate in the creator’s own environment.

7. Children

StreamClipper is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect, store, or process personal data from children under 13.

If a child under 13 is using the service, the creator is responsible for ensuring compliance with applicable laws and obtaining any required parental consent or supervision.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect service changes, legal requirements, or user needs. If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Continued use of StreamClipper after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to request deletion, or need support, contact us at argintmax12389@gmail.com.

This policy is written for the personal use case of the creator operating StreamClipper. It is not a general consumer-facing service policy for a public platform, and the creator remains responsible for the lawful and appropriate use of the app and any connected platforms.
